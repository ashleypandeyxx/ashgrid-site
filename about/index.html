What it does differently

Four things worth knowing
01

Measured against you

Every run opens by recording where you actually are. What comes back is your change from that point — not an average built from people who aren't you, running protocols you didn't.

02

Built for combinations

Nobody takes one thing. Run two or three together as a stack, and the hard part — telling what helped from what was along for the ride — is the problem AshGrid is built around.

03

Your wearable does the recording

Sleep, HRV and resting heart rate arrive from Apple Health or Health Connect: read-only, never written back, every value carrying its source. About a minute a day is left for you.

04

Nothing gets overwritten

A correction is a new entry pointing at the old one, results are versioned so a better method never rewrites your history, and your starting snapshot is fixed the moment it's taken. The record stays private unless you choose otherwise, and exports in full whenever you ask.
