The loop

Four steps, and the math stays honest.

No streaks to game, no scores that flatter you. Just a clean before, a structured middle, and a change you can trace.

01

Record a starting snapshot

One immutable reading of what you're about to observe — from a wearable where it can, from you where it can't. This is your reference constant.

SNAPSHOT
02

Run a structured protocol

Pick a real protocol — amount, timing, duration, formulation. Search before you create, so your run joins the map instead of fragmenting it.

PROTOCOL
03

Check in, about a minute a day

Confirm the protocol, answer a couple of fixed questions, let wearable metrics fill themselves in. Works offline; records instantly.

CHECK-IN
04

See your reported change

At the recommended duration you get an immutable result: your change vs the snapshot, the effect size, the uncertainty — plain-language, then the stats on demand.

RESULT
The method

It shows uncertainty instead of hiding it.

  • One-sample, one snapshot

    Each experiment day is compared with your single starting snapshot as a fixed reference — and the method discloses where nominal p-values can read optimistic.

  • Measured outcome ≠ your verdict

    The statistics and how you felt are kept as two separate signals. AshGrid can even surface when they disagree — never quietly overwrite one with the other.

  • Negatives and gaps count

    Missed days, no-effect runs, and things you noticed aren't buried. Insufficient data stays inconclusive — a reliability score is never dressed up as a probability.

  • Every number is traceable

    Oura, Apple Health, or you — each value carries its source. Results are versioned, so improving the algorithm never rewrites your history.

Community intelligence

Your run makes the map sharper.

When you contribute a de-identified result, it joins reliability-adjusted community observations — ordered by consistency, sample size, adherence and provenance, not by whoever shouted loudest.

  • The 30-user floor

    A combination finding only shows publicly when every compared group has at least 30 unique people — plus significance and a real effect. No sparse cross-filtered cohorts.

  • Association, stated as association

    Combination insights show sample size, uncertainty, and method version — described as observed patterns, never as a mechanism or a promise.

  • What didn't work is kept

    Failed and abandoned runs are first-class community data. They lower reliability rankings honestly instead of vanishing.

What AshGrid is — and isn't

Serious about being honest about limits.

A personal wellness observation platform for US adults. Not a diagnosis, not a prescriber, not a pharmacy, not a medical device.

Clear boundaries

AshGrid records personal observations. It does not decide that anything is safe or effective for you.

—No dosing, sourcing, or treatment for restricted substances
—Condition-like searches become non-diagnostic wellness contexts
—Urgent red-flag guidance surfaces before anything else

Privacy by design

Your health data works for your results and consented aggregates — and is never turned into a product to sell.

—No health-targeted ads, data brokers, or record licensing
—Wearables are read-only; raw provider records are never sold
—Contribution is opt-in, affirmative, and revocable

Built to last

Health-grade data discipline underneath the calm surface — because the data deserves it.

—Append-only events; corrections reference the prior, never erase
—Immutable snapshots and versioned, auditable results
—US data regions, minimum-necessary AI, no PHI in logs
